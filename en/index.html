Privacy policy

Unknown Number / Número Desconhecido (game for Android and the web) · effective November 24, 2026

Summary

Unknown Number does not collect, sell or share personal data. The game has no ads, no trackers and no usage analytics. Everything you do in the game is saved only on your device.

What is saved on your device

  • Story progress: the in-game conversations, your choices, the clues and the Case notebook.
  • Settings: language, text size, theme, sound, vibration, message pace and the nickname you type (the nickname is optional and only appears inside the story).
  • Endings already seen, to show how many of the endings you have found.
  • Date and time of your last session, to show a story recap when you come back after a few days.

This data stays in the app’s local storage and is not sent to anyone. To delete it: use “Start over” in the game (erases progress), clear the app’s data in Android settings, or uninstall the game (erases everything).

Permissions

  • Internet: declared by the app, but this version makes no connections: all story content ships inside the game.
  • Notifications (optional): to let you know when a character writes while the game is closed. Notifications are created on the device itself, with no server. You can decline or turn them off at any time.
  • Run at startup, keep awake and alarms: used only by the local notification feature, so that a notification already scheduled still works after the device restarts and shows up on time. They do not read or send any data.
  • Vibration: for message alerts, which can be turned off in the game settings.

Voice messages

The characters’ voice messages are read by your device’s text-to-speech feature. The text is processed by the speech service configured on your Android device, under that provider’s privacy policy. The game does not record or send your voice and does not use the microphone.

Optional account (turned off in this version)

The game has an optional account prepared for continuing the story on another device. It is not active in this version. If it is turned on in the future, this policy will be updated first, and the account will remain optional. In that case, only the email address or Google account identifier used to sign in and a copy of the game progress (including the nickname) would be stored on a database server, solely so you can continue where you left off. You will be able to request deletion of the account and that data.

Web version

If you play in a web browser, your progress is saved in the browser itself. The service that delivers the pages may log technical access data (such as IP address and browser type) for security, under its own policy. The game does not use tracking cookies.

Children

The game is a suspense story with jump scares and themes of disappearance and violence, without explicit scenes, and is not directed at children. It does not collect data from any player of any age.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date. For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact email shown on the game’s Google Play page.

Unknown Number is a work of fiction.
