Privacy policy

Unknown Number / Número Desconhecido (game for Android and the web) · effective November 27, 2026

Summary

Story 1 is free and can be played without an account. From Story 2 on, stories are paid: buying requires an account (e-mail or Google), and then we keep the account and purchase data described below. We do not sell data and the game has no ads and no trackers. It sends anonymous gameplay statistics to help improve the story. They are on by default, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings. Your progress is saved only on your device.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

  • What is sent: game events such as opening the game, starting and finishing an episode (with the play time), which option was picked at each story choice, the ending reached, playing a voice message, opening a transcript, zooming into a photo, changing the language, and the point in the story where a session stopped. Each event also carries the platform (Android or web browser), the language, the game version and two random codes: a device code, created by the game itself and kept only on your device, and a session code, new every time the game opens.
  • What is not sent: name, nickname, email, phone number, contacts, location, IP address, device or Google account identifiers, or the text of anything you read or reply. The device code is a randomly drawn number with no link to your device, to you or to any other data.
  • Why: only to understand how the story is played (where people stop, which choices they make, which endings they find) and to improve the game. The statistics are not used for advertising or to identify anyone.
  • Where it is stored: in a Cloudflare database (Cloudflare D1), used as an infrastructure provider. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). It is not sold or shared with third parties.
  • How long: events are deleted automatically after 13 months. After that, only a daily summary with totals remains, without the device code.
  • How to turn it off: in the game, open Settings → Privacy → “Send anonymous statistics”. When it is off, nothing else is sent and the events still waiting on your device are erased.
  • How to request deletion: Settings → Privacy shows your device code. Send that code to the developer contact email shown on the game’s Google Play page, and the events linked to it will be deleted.

What is saved on your device

  • Story progress: the in-game conversations, your choices, the clues and the Case notebook.
  • Settings: language, text size, theme, sound, vibration, message pace, your statistics choice and the nickname you type. The nickname is optional, only appears inside the story and is never sent.
  • Endings already seen, to show how many of the endings you have found.
  • Date and time of your last session, to show a story recap when you come back after a few days.
  • The random device code and the statistics queue, which waits for the next connection to be sent.

This data stays in the app’s local storage. To delete it, use “Start over” in the game (erases progress), clear the app’s data in Android settings, or uninstall the game (erases everything, including the random code).

Permissions

  • Internet: used to send the anonymous statistics, sign in, buy and download purchased stories. Story 1, including its voice recordings, ships inside the game.
  • Google Play billing: used only for buying stories in the app.
  • Notifications (optional): to let you know when a character writes while the game is closed. Notifications are created on the device itself, with no server. You can decline or turn them off at any time.
  • Run at startup, keep awake and alarms: used only by the local notification feature, so that a notification already scheduled still works after the device restarts and shows up on time. They do not read or send any data.
  • Vibration: for message alerts, which can be turned off in the game settings.

Voice messages

The characters’ voice messages are recordings that ship inside the game. When a line has no recording, it is read by your device’s text-to-speech feature, under that provider’s privacy policy. The game does not record or send your voice and does not use the microphone.

Account

An account is optional to play Story 1 and required to buy the other stories (so the purchase works on the website and in the app). To sign in we use your e-mail address (with a code sent by e-mail) or your Google account. We store the e-mail, an account identifier and, if you use sync, a copy of your game progress (including your nickname). This data is kept in Supabase, a database service used as an infrastructure provider.

Purchases

  • What we keep for each purchase: the account that bought it, the story, the amount, the payment method (Pix, card or Google Play), the status (pending, approved, declined, expired, refunded), the dates and the order number at Mercado Pago or Google Play. This is used to unlock the story on your account, show your purchase history, handle refund requests and meet tax obligations.
  • On the website, payment is processed by Mercado Pago on behalf of Meu Bairro Buritis, the seller. Card details are typed into Mercado Pago's own form and never pass through our servers; with Pix, you pay in your bank app. Mercado Pago receives the data needed for the payment (such as e-mail, CPF when requested and card details) and handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • In the Android app, payment is made through Google Play, under Google's privacy policy. From Google we only receive the purchase confirmation (product, status and order number) and a code that links the purchase to your game account without revealing your e-mail to Google.
  • Purchased content: is downloaded to your device after the purchase, so you can play offline. If the purchase is refunded, charged back or disputed, the story is no longer unlocked and the copy on the device is deleted at the next connection.
  • How long: purchase records are kept for 5 years for tax and accounting obligations, even if the account is deleted earlier (in that case without the e-mail).
  • Who we share it with: only the necessary processors: Mercado Pago (website payments), Google (app payments and Google sign-in), Supabase (account and purchases), Resend (sending the sign-in code and purchase confirmation e-mails) and Cloudflare (website servers and content delivery). We only send those two kinds of e-mail: no marketing. None of them receives the data for its own advertising.

To request deletion of your account or a copy of your data, use the developer contact e-mail shown on the game's Google Play page. You have the rights provided by Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD).

Web version and website

If you play in a web browser, your progress is saved in the browser itself and the anonymous statistics work as described above. The website and the game are delivered by Cloudflare, which may log technical access data (such as IP address and browser type) for security, under its own policy. Visits to the website pages are counted by Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies. The game does not use tracking cookies. The IP address is never stored with the game statistics.

Children

The game is a suspense story with jump scares and themes of disappearance and violence, without explicit scenes, and is not directed at children. The anonymous statistics do not identify anyone, of any age.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date. For privacy questions or requests, including deleting your statistics, use the developer contact email shown on the game’s Google Play page.

Unknown Number is a work of fiction.
